# Production workflow

State the film in three sentences: setup, transformation and payoff. Assign one focal idea per shot and one recurring token across the film. Define motion causally—anticipation, action, follow-through, settle—not as independent decorative wobble.

Storyboard columns: start/end, focal subject, action, camera, transition, audio cue and validation frame. Build one look still first. Then build end-to-end in small batches and inspect contact sheets plus every-frame strips around difficult transitions.
