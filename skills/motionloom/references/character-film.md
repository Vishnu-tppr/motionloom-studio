# Character film

Write the character's want, obstacle and emotional change. Keep construction, proportions, palette and distinguishing marks stable. Prepare a small model sheet: front, three-quarter, side, five expressions and key props.

Act from the torso and gaze before adding limbs. Use anticipation, readable silhouette, overlap, drag, follow-through, overshoot and settle. Blinks should support thought or impact, not run as a mechanical timer. Avoid independent sine-wave movement on every part.

Review face/hand/foot crops and frame strips for contacts, turns and takes. If a pose cannot be read as a still silhouette, fix the pose rather than adding motion.
